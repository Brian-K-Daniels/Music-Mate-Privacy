Music Mate

About page and user guide

Welcome

Music Mate helps you learn to read music and play your instrument with better pitch and rhythm. It listens through the microphone on your phone or tablet and shows feedback on a musical staff as you play.

It is made for children who are just starting, and it is also useful for parents, teachers, and adult musicians who want clear practice and steady progress.

You cannot break anything by exploring. If you ever want to start fresh, use Reset and Options / Factory Defaults in the menu ((☰) at the very top left of the screen).

Getting Started

Here is a simple way to begin and succeed on the first try:

  1. Open the menu (☰) and choose Home.
  2. Tap Instrument and pick the instrument you play.
  3. Set Level to something comfortable (many beginners start near Level 1).
  4. If you do not see a Start button then swipe up near the middle of the screen
  5. Tap ▶ Start. Music Mate sets up practice for that level and opens the Music page.
  6. Tap the green GO circle in the title bar so the app can listen. (If it instead shows Stop in a red box then carry on; it is listening.)
  7. Play the yellow note on the staff. When it turns green, play the next note.
  8. Keep going to the end of the exercise.

Tips for a good first session: Find a quiet place. Hold your instrument ready before you tap GO. Play the notes in order, not ahead of the yellow note.

Getting Around

Tap the menu button (☰) at the top left to open these pages:

  • Home — choose instrument and level, then start practice.
  • What to Play — choose tunes, scales, arpeggios, By Level, Random, or Tuner, and auto-repeat options.
  • Music — the main practice screen with the staff, listening, and playback.
  • Settings — tempo, colors, rhythm, mastery rules, and practice switches.
  • Reset and Options — restore factory defaults or your own saved custom defaults.
  • Settings Advanced — practice mix, level-up rules, and detailed audio settings (for teachers and experienced users).
  • My Progress — review your note and session history.
  • About — this guide, search, and Premium purchase.

The back arrow (←) returns you to Music.

On long pages, drag to scroll. A quick flick moves faster through lists.

What to Play

Use What to Play to choose your exercise before or between sessions. There are four pickers: Tunes, Scales, Arpeggios, and Other. Choosing one mode clears the others so only one practice choice is active.

Instrument and key can also be set on the Music page. For transposing instruments (such as B♭ clarinet or E♭ alto sax), the staff shows written music for your instrument. The concert key appears in parentheses when that helps.

By Level

Under Other, choose By Level. Music Mate builds practice that matches your current level — notes, keys, rhythms, and difficulty grow as your level rises.

Tunes

Play short pieces written with real rhythms and bar lines:

  • Mary Had a Little Lamb
  • Ode to Joy
  • Minuet in G (simplified)
  • Half through Sixteenth Notes — a rhythm-focused exercise

Scales

Scale mode shows the key signature and walks ascending and descending so you can read, hear, and play in key. Free edition includes Major and Harmonic Minor. Premium unlocks Melodic Minor, Natural Minor, modes (Dorian, Phrygian, Lydian, Mixolydian, Locrian), pentatonic, blues, enigmatic, chromatic, and more.

Arpeggios

Arpeggio mode practices broken chord patterns such as major and minor triads and seventh chords. More arpeggio types unlock as your level increases.Choose the starting note and the type of chord you want to practise. Music Mate will show the correct written notes for your instrument.

Random

Under Other, choose Random for a fresh exercise built from your key, scale, note range, and accidental settings. Notes you have already mastered can be left out so you spend more time on notes that still need work.

Tip: Random works best when a scale is selected. The page will warn you if Random is on without a helpful scale choice.

Tuner

Under Other, choose Tuner. Play any note. Music Mate shows the nearest note name, how sharp or flat you are (in cents), and the frequency. Tuner does not run a note-by-note exercise like GO practice on a tune.

Repeat options

After you finish a session, Music Mate can start again after a short pause:

  • Repeat — for scales and similar modes, start another session automatically.
  • New Each Time (Random) — create a new random exercise next.
  • Repeat Same (Random) — practice the same random exercise again.

Music Page

This is where you play. The title bar shows Music and a short status message.

Listening and playback

Tap the green GO circle to start listening. While listening, it becomes a red Stop button.

Tap the green Play button near the staff to hear the exercise. During playback it becomes red Stop. Use Play to learn how the exercise should sound before you play it yourself.

Staff feedback

  • Yellow — the note you should play now.
  • Green — played correctly.
  • Red — incorrect attempt (still pending).
  • Black or dim — upcoming notes.

If Show Conductor Cues is on in Settings, red arrows can appear above the staff on conducted beats (not in Tuner).

Level buttons

When you are practicing with a level (and not in Tuner), buttons let you change level by −10, −5, −1, +1, +5, or +10. The status area can show progress toward the next level, such as sessions completed and accuracy scores.

When you finish

If you earn a level-up, a celebration banner appears (for example: Great job! You advanced to Level …). Ordinary sessions update your progress in the status area without that banner.

Levels and Progress

Levels run from 1 to 100. Home shows a short description of each band:

  • 1–10 Beginner — More notes, C major, quarter notes
  • 11–20 New keys — More keys, longer note values
  • 21–30 Rests — Rests and natural minor
  • 31–40 Minor & 16ths — Minor scales and sixteenth notes
  • 41–50 Syncopation — Simple off-beat rhythms
  • 51–60 Modes & blues — Modes and blues sounds
  • 61–70 Full syncopation — Full syncopation
  • 71–100 Mastery — Wider range, speed, and chromatic notes

When you tap ▶ Start on Home, Music Mate applies level-based settings (keys, rhythms, note range, and more) and begins a fresh window for counting level-up sessions.

Automatic level-up uses rules from Settings Advanced (sessions required, pitch accuracy, timing, overall accuracy, and minimum notes per session). Qualifying sessions are counted per instrument. The level number you see is shared for the app. Levels do not go down by themselves.

How Practice Works

Play each yellow note in order. When Music Mate accepts the correct pitch, that note turns green and the next note becomes current.

Wrong pitches turn red until you play the note correctly later. Playing too early can also count as a timing problem and flash red without advancing.

From about Level 21 upward, timing matters more for your overall score and for whether a note counts as mastered. Below that, pitch accuracy is the main focus for mastery.

In Settings, Listening starts when music appears can begin listening for you when the Music page opens (not used in Tuner).

Mastery and Random

In Settings under Mastery of notes, choose how Music Mate decides a note is mastered:

  • % Correct — enough attempts and a high enough percent correct (and related rules).
  • Streak — enough correct plays in a row.

Mastered notes can be left out of Random practice so you keep improving the notes that still need work. Omit below MsAvg can keep slower notes in the pool when you want to emphasize response speed (set to 0 to turn that rule off).

My Progress

Open My Progress and choose a database:

  • Note — each written note: Correct, Wrong, % Correct, average response time (MsAvg), Streak, and Mastered (Yes when current mastery rules say so).
  • Session — one row per session, including Level, accuracy scores, Key, Instrument, and What (a short label for what you practiced, such as Random or By Level).
  • Child Results — practice results used for automatic level-up counting. Use Clear All carefully if you want to reset that progress window.

Tap a column heading to sort. Use Clear All only when you really want to erase the selected database. Refresh reloads the tables.

Settings and Customization

Display

Colors selection opens a color picker for backgrounds, text, buttons, pickers, and more. Show note names can be Current only, All notes, or Off.

Tempo

One BPM slider sets practice and playback tempo (about 30–200).

Music

  • Accidental % — how often Random adds sharps or flats (above 0 requires Premium).
  • Lowest note / Highest note — practice range (free edition is limited to about C4–F5).
  • Rhythm mode, Smallest note, Syncopation, and Time signature — how written rhythms are built.

Action switches

  • Listening starts when music appears.
  • Show Conductor Cues
  • Number of latest attempts per note to keep
  • Collect session statistics

Reset and Options

  • Factory Defaults — restore original settings (including advanced options and theme colors).
  • Make Custom Default Settings equal to Current Settings. — save your current setup.
  • Custom Defaults — restore your saved setup.

The active defaults button is highlighted so you can see which set is in effect.

Settings Advanced

Practice composition % balances Tunes, Random, Scales, and Arpeggios for By Level style practice.

Level Advancement Criteria control how many good sessions and what accuracy you need to move up.

Advanced Audio & Pitch Settings (Pitch Tolerance, RMS Threshold, Cooldown, Pitch Offset) help teachers fine-tune detection. Most users can leave these alone.

You can also limit how large the session database may grow.

Premium

Music Mate has a free edition and a Premium edition.

Free edition

  • Keys: C, F, B♭, G, and D
  • Scales: Major and Harmonic Minor
  • Note range limited to about C4–F5
  • Accidental % stays at 0 in Random

Premium edition

Premium unlocks more keys and scales, wider note ranges, accidentals in Random, and related options. It is a one-time purchase that includes future app updates.

If you choose a Premium-only option while free, a message appears. An adult can buy Premium or continue with free settings.

On this About page, tap Get Premium to purchase. When Premium is active you will see: ⭐ You have all premium privileges.

Tips for Success

  • Start at a level that feels doable. Winning small sessions builds confidence.
  • Use Play to hear the exercise before you play it.
  • Watch for the yellow note — that is your target — then enjoy the green when you get it right.
  • Practice a little every day. My Progress helps you see improvement over time.
  • Keep the room as quiet as you can so the microphone can hear you clearly.
  • Celebrate progress. Every correct note is a step forward. Come back tomorrow and try again!

Things to Know

Pitch detection works well in normal conditions, but no app is perfect. Very soft or very loud rooms, wrong-octave attempts, or noisy backgrounds can affect results. Music Mate keeps improving.
